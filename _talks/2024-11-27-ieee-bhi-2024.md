I attended the IEEE Conference on Biomedical and Health Informatics in Kragujevac, Serbia, which brought together researchers working on technology-driven healthcare solutions.

The presentations covered real-time annotation systems, advanced modeling in medical imaging and informatics, and applications such as molecular transport analysis and predictive annotation tools. Thank you to the organizers and speakers for such a collaborative and enriching environment.
