I attended GITEX Europe 2025 in Berlin as an AI developer and early-career data scientist. The event covered how fast innovation is moving across healthcare, cybersecurity, and quantum computing.

The conversations about AI diagnostics, robot-assisted care, and personalized medicine stood out most. I also joined sessions on AI security and governance, including "The Future: Cybersecurity Will Be AI vs. AI", "Quantum Innovation: The AWS Approach", and "Winning Strategies for AI Governance & Red Teaming".

My main takeaway: build AI responsibly, think holistically, and keep humans at the center, especially in sensitive fields like healthcare.
