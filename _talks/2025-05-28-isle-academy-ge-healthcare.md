Together with my EMMBIOME cohort, I took part in a hands-on day at ISLE Academy with the GE HealthCare team, exploring the future of medical technology, from advanced healthcare software and precision imaging to the capabilities of the Da Vinci robotic surgical systems.

The hands-on demos and conversations gave a close look at how innovation is shaping the next generation of patient care.
