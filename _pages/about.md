I am a Machine Learning Engineer and Biomedical AI Researcher with 3+ years of hands-on experience across industry, clinical, and academic environments. I specialize in vision-language models, medical image analysis, NLP pipelines, and end-to-end AI system deployment.

I recently completed my Master's thesis research at Erasmus Medical Center, fine-tuning Gemma vision-language models for liver lesion diagnosis and deploying reproducible ML pipelines and statistical analysis frameworks for clinical AI validation. I am a published researcher with expertise in medical image segmentation, OCT vessel classification, responsible/explainable AI, and regulatory AI systems, with a strong interest in applying AI and data science methods to real-world healthcare data.

Education
======
* M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering (Erasmus Mundus EMMBIOME), University of Kragujevac / University of Patras / University of Medicine and Pharmacy Grigore T. Popa, 2024–2026
* M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, University of Jordan, 2023–2024
* B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering, Jordan University of Science and Technology, 2018–2023

Research interests
======
* Vision-language models for medical imaging
* Medical image segmentation & classification (MRI, CT, OCT)
* Clinical NLP and speech processing
* Responsible & explainable AI
* Regulatory AI systems for healthcare
